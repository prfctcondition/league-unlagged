# League Unlagged — Design System & Color Palette

**Author:** `prfctCondition`

This document defines the visual guidelines and color palette for League Unlagged (`src/theme.rs`).

## 1. Core Principles
1. **Light Theme (Default)**:
   - **Base**: Clean whites, frosted glass, and light slates (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`, `#FFFFFF`).
   - **Text**: Deep slate and charcoal (`#0F172A`, `#1E293B`, `#475569`, `#64748B`).
   - **Primary Accent & Active Borders**: Deep Navy (`#1B2C54`) with Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) contrast text.
2. **Dark Theme**:
   - **Monochromatic Black-and-White Palette**: Strictly black, dark grays, and white.
   - **Base**: OLED Pure Black (`#000000`), Obsidian glass surfaces (`#060608`, `#08080B`, `#0C0C10`).
   - **Text**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`), body (`#F4F4F5`), secondary (`rgba(255,255,255,0.65)`), muted (`rgba(255,255,255,0.40)`).
   - **Primary Accent**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with Pitch Black (`#000000`) contrast text.
